This page archives Doppler radar images from the India Meteorological Department's Karaikal radar station, showing rain over South India and Northern Sri Lanka. IMD only keeps the last ~3 hours online, so this site saves the history for day-wise replay. Note: some noise / false echoes to the west and south-east, but rainfall over the Northern Province is accurate.
